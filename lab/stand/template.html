The styles

StyleLikeDetailed, young to oldSimple

Triangles per tree. Each style has a shape for each stage of growth: spires gain layers, saplings are thin and sparse, palms keep full-size fronds and grow a trunk. Each tree is then stretched up to 10% either way so no two match.

How they stay light

  • Solid shapes, no see-through leaf pictures. See-through leaves are what usually make trees slow.
  • A detailed shape per growth stage near you; one simple shape per style beyond, a handful of triangles.
  • One draw call per shape, however many trees.
  • A tree is 20 bytes: where it stands, its height, its width, which way it faces, a shade.
  • Trees are sent nearest first, so the card skips hidden ones cheaply. The ground is drawn after them for the same reason.
  • The list is refreshed only after you move 20 m or turn. Standing still costs nothing.
  • Beyond the last tree, the forest is the ground's colour, as in the app now.
