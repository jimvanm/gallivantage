      Rules for each kind of road

      Kindkm/hsteepest %hilltopdipbed m

      Bed: how far a road may ride above the ground to stay smooth. Past that, a slow road follows the bumps; 0 means none (motorways and railways bridge instead). Hilltop and dip: how gently the road must round off, in metres of road for each 1% change in steepness. Bigger is gentler. At 120 km/h a hilltop needs about 95, so a driver sees far enough over it.
